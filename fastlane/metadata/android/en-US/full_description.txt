A replacement phone app for Android: dialer, contacts, call history and a full call screen, with an arcade twist on answering.

<b>The call screen</b>

An incoming call gives you two things to grab, each with one destination:

* Drag the chomper onto the green handset to answer. It eats the pellets on the way.
* Drag the skull onto the chomper to reject.

Two independent drags means a fast diagonal swipe can never lock onto the wrong action.

<b>Features</b>

* Recents grouped per number, with an expandable "+2" for repeat calls
* A "Missed" filter that hides anything you have already called back
* Horizontal favorites strip with drag-to-reorder
* Per-contact notes, shown on screen the next time that person calls
* Per-contact ringtones, including your own music
* Reject with a canned SMS reply, or "remind me in X minutes"
* System-wide number blocking, using Android's own blocked-number list
* Pick the call audio output: speaker, earpiece, wired headset or any connected Bluetooth device, by name
* Motion shortcuts: raise to ear to answer, flip face down to silence, power key to silence and blank the screen
* Proximity blanking during a call
* Contacts import and export as .vcf
* Six themes for the call screen: chomper, plain, rocket, balloon, football and cat
* A call screen background you pick: six gradients or your own photo, with a blur slider and a live preview
* Speed dial: long-press 1-9 on the keypad, with an optional confirmation
* Five selectable launcher icons, light and dark themes
* A first-run tour that lets you practise the gesture on a fake call
* Call recording on rooted devices only - without root the button is hidden, because Android gives ordinary apps nothing but silence during a call

<b>What it deliberately does not do</b>

* No internet access. There is no INTERNET permission at all.
* No writing to your call log. Only READ_CALL_LOG is requested.
* No recording of the other party. Since Android 10 an ordinary app cannot reach the call audio stream, so the record button stays hidden unless the device is rooted.
